> The order below is a suggested workflow. The required content and effects come from the Classroom instructions.

2. [ ] Create a poster document in the selected software. Suggested: follow any document settings that the teacher provides later; none are visible in Classroom.
3. [ ] Make the chosen college event or activity the clear focus.
4. [ ] Add the event date, time, venue, and special instructions. Make them clear and easy to find.
5. [ ] Integrate the college logo or relevant branding element.
6. [ ] Add relevant images or illustrations.
