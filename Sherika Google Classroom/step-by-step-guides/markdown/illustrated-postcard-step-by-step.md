## Suggested workflow

> Everything in this section is a suggestion for organizing the work. Classroom does not specify these stages.

### 1. Interpret the brief

- [ ] Reread the Required by Classroom section.
- [ ] Write the chosen movement in the blank above.
- [ ] Note visual features of that movement that you may explore.
- [ ] Write a short concept or narrative in your own words.
- [ ] Decide what the postcard will communicate.

### 2. Research and cite

- [ ] Collect references that help you understand the chosen movement.
- [ ] Record enough source information to cite each reference.
- [ ] Separate references from your original work.
- [ ] Decide which references will appear in the process documentation.

### 3. Explore independently

- [ ] Make multiple rough ideas or studies.
- [ ] Test how design elements and principles can support the chosen movement.
- [ ] Compare the ideas for content, communication, concept, and narrative.
- [ ] Choose one direction to develop.

### 4. Make the analog work

- [ ] Use the selected analog tool or tools.
- [ ] Record useful stages of the analog process.
- [ ] Preserve evidence that shows your own exploration and decisions.
- [ ] Prepare the analog material for the digital stage.

### 5. Develop the work digitally

- [ ] Bring the analog material into the selected digital tool or tools.
- [ ] Develop the composition while keeping the chosen movement visible in the design approach.
- [ ] If the work includes type-based elements, check that the font reflects the movement.
- [ ] Record important digital stages, tools, and decisions.
- [ ] Check that the final result answers the illustrated-postcard brief.

### 6. Build the process PDF

- [ ] Arrange the documentation in a clear structure.
- [ ] Show the process, including the combination of analog and digital tools.
- [ ] Show the final outcome clearly.
- [ ] Include citations and references.
- [ ] Use accurate digital imaging nomenclature when describing tools and processes.
- [ ] Check that the document communicates how the concept or narrative developed.

### 7. Export and name the file

- [ ] Export the documentation and final outcome as a PDF.
- [ ] Follow the nomenclature written in Classroom: EA1A_Student Name_Section_Fall2026.
- [ ] Replace “Student Name” and “Section” with your own information.
- My exact final filename: ______
