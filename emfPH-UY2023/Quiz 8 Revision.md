## Quiz 8 Questions

### Quiz 8 #1 — Parallel plates: voltage and surface charge density
Two very large parallel metal plates are $d=2.00\,\mathrm{mm}$ apart and carry equal, opposite uniform charges. The potential difference is $\Delta V=10.0\,\mathrm V$. Find the surface charge density on the positive plate.
![[PH-UY 2023 F26 HW08 - PH-UY 2023_ ELECTRICITY, MAGNETISM, & FLUIDS (F26), Fall 2026 _ WebAssign_files/16-dwb-002a.png]]
?
$E=\Delta V/d=5.00\times10^3\,\mathrm{V/m}$ and $\sigma=\epsilon_0E$.
$\boxed{\sigma=44.3\,\mathrm{nC/m^2}}.$

## Homework 8 Questions

### HW08 #1 — Series and parallel capacitor rules
For capacitors in series, state what is shared and give the equivalent capacitance. Repeat for capacitors in parallel.
![[PH-UY 2023 F26 HW08 - PH-UY 2023_ ELECTRICITY, MAGNETISM, & FLUIDS (F26), Fall 2026 _ WebAssign_files/16-dwb-002c.png]]
![[PH-UY 2023 F26 HW08 - PH-UY 2023_ ELECTRICITY, MAGNETISM, & FLUIDS (F26), Fall 2026 _ WebAssign_files/16-dwb-002d.png]]
?
Series: $\boxed{Q_1=Q_2=\cdots}$ and $\boxed{1/C_{\rm eq}=\sum 1/C_i}$.
Parallel: $\boxed{V_1=V_2=\cdots}$ and $\boxed{C_{\rm eq}=\sum C_i}$.

### HW08 #2 — Spherical capacitor
A spherical capacitor has outer-shell radius $b=7.20\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$, capacitance $C=1.70\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm F$, and voltage drop $6.00\,\mathrm V$. Find the inner radius, the positive-shell charge, and the inner-shell surface-charge-density magnitude.
?
$C=4\pi\epsilon_0ab/(b-a)\Rightarrow\boxed{a=1.26\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm m}$.
$Q=C\Delta V=\boxed{1.02\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm C}$; $\sigma=Q/(4\pi a^2)=\boxed{5.11\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{C/m^2}}$.

### HW08 #3 — Capacitors in parallel
Two capacitors, $C_1=6.00\,\mu\mathrm F$ and $C_2=13.0\,\mu\mathrm F$, are connected in parallel to a $9.00\,\mathrm V$ battery. Find $C_{\rm eq}$, the voltage across each capacitor, and both stored charges.
?
$C_{\rm eq}=C_1+C_2=\boxed{19.0\,\mu\mathrm F}$; $\boxed{V_1=V_2=9.00\,\mathrm V}$.
$Q_1=C_1V=\boxed{54.0\,\mu\mathrm C}$; $Q_2=C_2V=\boxed{117\,\mu\mathrm C}$.

### HW08 #4 — Capacitors in series
Two capacitors, $C_1=6.00\,\mu\mathrm F$ and $C_2=10.0\,\mu\mathrm F$, are connected in series to a $9.00\,\mathrm V$ battery. Find the equivalent capacitance, the charge on each, and the voltage across each.
?
$C_{\rm eq}=(1/6.00+1/10.0)^{-1}=\boxed{3.75\,\mu\mathrm F}$.
$Q=C_{\rm eq}V=\boxed{33.8\,\mu\mathrm C}$ on each; $V_1=Q/C_1=\boxed{5.63\,\mathrm V}$, $V_2=Q/C_2=\boxed{3.38\,\mathrm V}$.

### HW08 #5 — Charge redistribution after switching
$C_1=5.00\,\mu\mathrm F$ is charged to $19.0\,\mathrm V$, then disconnected from the battery and connected in parallel to an initially uncharged $C_2=8.00\,\mu\mathrm F$. Find the initial charge on $C_1$ and the final charge on each capacitor.
![[PH-UY 2023 F26 HW08 - PH-UY 2023_ ELECTRICITY, MAGNETISM, & FLUIDS (F26), Fall 2026 _ WebAssign_files/26-p-024.gif]]
?
$Q_i=C_1V=(5.00)(19.0)=\boxed{95.0\,\mu\mathrm C}$.
$V_f=Q_i/(C_1+C_2)=7.31\,\mathrm V$; $Q_{1f}=\boxed{36.5\,\mu\mathrm C}$, $Q_{2f}=\boxed{58.5\,\mu\mathrm C}$.

### HW08 #6 — Three capacitors in a combination
$C_1=2.3\,\mu\mathrm F$ is in series with $C_2=1.8\,\mu\mathrm F$ and $C_3=5.1\,\mu\mathrm F$ in parallel. Find the equivalent capacitance.

### HW08 #7 — Energy stored in a capacitor
A $15.0\,\mu\mathrm F$ capacitor is connected to a $220\,\mathrm V$ battery. Find the maximum energy stored after the switch is closed.
![[PH-UY 2023 F26 HW08 - PH-UY 2023_ ELECTRICITY, MAGNETISM, & FLUIDS (F26), Fall 2026 _ WebAssign_files/16-dwb-002f.png]]
?
$U=\tfrac12CV^2=\tfrac12(15.0\times10^{-6})(220)^2=\boxed{0.363\,\mathrm J}$.

### HW08 #8 — Isolated capacitors and changing plate separation
Two identical capacitors, each initially $C$ and charged to $\Delta V$, are disconnected from the battery and connected in parallel with like-sign plates joined. The separation of one capacitor is then doubled. Find the final voltage and total energy.
?
$Q=2C\Delta V$ stays fixed; doubling $d$ gives $C_f=C+C/2=3C/2$.
$\Delta V_f=Q/C_f=\boxed{4\Delta V/3}$ and $U_f=\tfrac12C_fV_f^2=\boxed{4C(\Delta V)^2/3}$.

### HW08 #9 — Removing a dielectric from an isolated capacitor
A $2.36\,\mathrm{nF}$ capacitor is charged to $100\,\mathrm V$, isolated, and has its Pyrex dielectric ($\kappa=5.6$) removed. Find the final voltage and the work required.
?
$Q$ is fixed, so $C_f=C_i/\kappa$ and $V_f=\kappa V_i=\boxed{560\,\mathrm V}$.
$W=\Delta U=\boxed{54.3\,\mu\mathrm J}$.

### HW08 #10 — Inserting a dielectric after disconnecting the battery
A parallel-plate capacitor has $d=1.56\,\mathrm{cm}$, $A=25.0\,\mathrm{cm^2}$, and is charged to $230\,\mathrm V$. The battery is removed, then the capacitor is immersed in water with $\kappa=80.0$. Find the charge before and after, final capacitance and voltage, and energy change.
?
$C_i=\epsilon_0A/d=1.43\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm F$ and $Q_i=C_iV_i=\boxed{326\,\mathrm{pC}}$.
Battery removed $\Rightarrow Q_f=\boxed{326\,\mathrm{pC}}$; $C_f=\kappa C_i=\boxed{1.14\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm F}$; $V_f=Q_f/C_f=\boxed{2.88\,\mathrm V}$.
$\Delta U=\boxed{-37.1\,\mathrm{nJ}}$.
