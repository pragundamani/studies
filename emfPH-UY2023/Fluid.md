>[!info]- What is a fluid
>Something with fixed mass but not fixed volume
> We only deal with *ideal fluids* (big enough to not be molecular but small enough to do calculus)
> the fluids flow laminarly, are incompressible and friction is ignored
> 

Pressure in terms of force $F$ and area $A$ :: $P(x)=\frac{F}{A}$
Relation of $\text{force and area}$ :: $F \propto A$
pressure at any 2 points :: $P(Y)=P(X)\times \rho gh$
