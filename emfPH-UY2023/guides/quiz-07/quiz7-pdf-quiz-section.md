## PDF Quiz Questions

### PDF Quiz #2 — Point where the electric field vanishes
A charge $-8Q$ is fixed at the origin, and a charge $+Q$ is fixed at $(d,0)$.
Find the $x$-coordinate where the net electric field on the $x$-axis vanishes.
![[../../assets/quiz-07/quiz7-pdf-q2-electric-field.png]]
?
The fields cannot cancel between the charges because both point to the left there.
For $x>d$, the fields oppose, so
$$\frac{kQ}{(x-d)^2}=\frac{8kQ}{x^2}.$$
Thus $x=\sqrt8(x-d)$ and
$$x=\frac{\sqrt8}{\sqrt8-1}d\approx1.55d.$$
Therefore, $\boxed{x=+1.55d}$, choice $\boxed{\text{b}}$.

### PDF Quiz #3 — Zero work and electric potential
A charge $-8Q$ is fixed at the origin, and a charge $+Q$ is fixed at $(d,0)$.
A third charge $+19Q$ is brought from infinity to a point $P=(0,y)$ on the positive $y$-axis.
Find $y$ if the work required to bring the third charge in is zero.
![[../../assets/quiz-07/quiz7-pdf-q3-electric-potential.png]]
?
Zero external work requires $\Delta U=(19Q)V(P)=0$, so the potential from the fixed charges must be zero.
At $P$,
$$V(P)=kQ\left(-\frac{8}{y}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{d^2+y^2}}\right).$$
Because $\sqrt{d^2+y^2}>y$, the positive term is less than $1/y$ and cannot cancel $-8/y$.
Therefore, no such point exists on the positive $y$-axis, choice $\boxed{\text{e}}$.
