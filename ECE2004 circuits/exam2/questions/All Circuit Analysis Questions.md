## KVL–KCL Questions

**1.** Find the series current and both downward branch currents. ![[../images/Q01-kvl-kcl-basic.png|800]] *An $18\ \mathrm{V}$ source is positive at the top. Its top terminal connects through $3\ \Omega$ to node A. Resistors of $6\ \Omega$ and $3\ \Omega$ connect from A to the bottom reference node. The series arrow points right; both branch arrows point downward.*
?
Let node A be $V_A$ and take the labeled directions as positive. KVL gives $18-3I-V_A=0$. KCL and Ohm’s law give $I=I_6+I_3=V_A/6+V_A/3=V_A/2$. Hence $18-3(V_A/2)-V_A=0$, so $V_A=7.20\ \mathrm{V}$. Therefore $I=3.60\ \mathrm{A}$ right, $I_6=1.20\ \mathrm{A}$ downward, and $I_3=2.40\ \mathrm{A}$ downward.

**2.** Write one node equation for A and use it to check KCL numerically. ![[../images/Q01-kvl-kcl-basic.png|800]] *An $18\ \mathrm{V}$ source is positive at the top. Its top terminal connects through $3\ \Omega$ to node A. Resistors of $6\ \Omega$ and $3\ \Omega$ connect from A to the bottom reference node. The series arrow points right; both branch arrows point downward.*
?
With currents leaving A positive, $\frac{V_A-18}{3}+\frac{V_A}{6}+\frac{V_A}{3}=0$. Multiplying by $6$ gives $2V_A-36+V_A+2V_A=0$, so $V_A=7.20\ \mathrm{V}$. Numerically, current entering is $(18-7.2)/3=3.60\ \mathrm{A}$ and current leaving is $7.2/6+7.2/3=3.60\ \mathrm{A}$.

**3.** Find the power of every element and verify conservation of power. ![[../images/Q01-kvl-kcl-basic.png|800]] *An $18\ \mathrm{V}$ source is positive at the top. Its top terminal connects through $3\ \Omega$ to node A. Resistors of $6\ \Omega$ and $3\ \Omega$ connect from A to the bottom reference node. The series arrow points right; both branch arrows point downward.*
?
From KVL and KCL, $I=3.60\ \mathrm{A}$, $I_6=1.20\ \mathrm{A}$, and $I_3=2.40\ \mathrm{A}$. With positive power defined as absorbed, $P_{3\Omega,\mathrm{series}}=(3.6)^2(3)=38.88\ \mathrm{W}$, $P_{6\Omega}=(1.2)^2(6)=8.64\ \mathrm{W}$, and $P_{3\Omega,\mathrm{branch}}=(2.4)^2(3)=17.28\ \mathrm{W}$. Source current leaves its positive terminal, so $P_{18\mathrm{V}}=-(18)(3.6)=-64.80\ \mathrm{W}$; it delivers $64.80\ \mathrm{W}$. The check is $38.88+8.64+17.28-64.80=0\ \mathrm{W}$.

**4.** Find node voltage A and the two resistor currents. ![[../images/Q02-kvl-kcl-power.png|800]] *A $24\ \mathrm{V}$ source is positive at the top and fixes the left node. A $4\ \Omega$ resistor connects it to node A. An $8\ \Omega$ resistor connects A to reference. A $2\ \mathrm{A}$ source points upward from reference to A. Resistor arrows point right through $4\ \Omega$ and downward through $8\ \Omega$.*
?
Take currents leaving A as positive. KCL gives $\frac{V_A-24}{4}+\frac{V_A}{8}-2=0$. Thus $2V_A-48+V_A-16=0$ and $V_A=64/3=21.33\ \mathrm{V}$. Therefore $i_4=(24-V_A)/4=2/3\ \mathrm{A}$ right and $i_8=V_A/8=8/3\ \mathrm{A}$ downward.

**5.** Classify each source as absorbing or delivering power and verify the power balance. ![[../images/Q02-kvl-kcl-power.png|800]] *A $24\ \mathrm{V}$ source is positive at the top and fixes the left node. A $4\ \Omega$ resistor connects it to node A. An $8\ \Omega$ resistor connects A to reference. A $2\ \mathrm{A}$ source points upward from reference to A. Resistor arrows point right through $4\ \Omega$ and downward through $8\ \Omega$.*
?
Using $V_A=64/3\ \mathrm{V}$, the resistors absorb $P_{4\Omega}=(2/3)^2(4)=16/9\ \mathrm{W}$ and $P_{8\Omega}=(8/3)^2(8)=512/9\ \mathrm{W}$. The voltage-source current leaves its positive terminal, so $P_{24\mathrm{V}}=-24(2/3)=-16\ \mathrm{W}$; it delivers. The current source enters its negative terminal, so $P_{2\mathrm{A}}=-(64/3)(2)=-128/3\ \mathrm{W}$; it delivers. The check is $16/9+512/9-16-128/3=0\ \mathrm{W}$.

**6.** Use nodal analysis to find both node voltages and the current through the linking resistor. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-two-node.png|800]] *Nodes A and B share a $2\ \Omega$ resistor. A has a $4\ \Omega$ resistor to reference and a $3\ \mathrm{A}$ source pointing upward from reference. B has a $6\ \Omega$ resistor to reference and a $1\ \mathrm{A}$ source pointing upward from reference. The bottom wire is reference.*
?
Take currents leaving each node as positive. The equations are $\frac{V_A}{4}+\frac{V_A-V_B}{2}-3=0$ and $\frac{V_B}{6}+\frac{V_B-V_A}{2}-1=0$. Equivalently, $3V_A-2V_B=12$ and $-3V_A+4V_B=6$. Solving gives $V_A=10.0\ \mathrm{V}$ and $V_B=9.0\ \mathrm{V}$. The link current is $(V_A-V_B)/2=0.500\ \mathrm{A}$ from A to B.

**7.** Find the power status of both current sources and check total power. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-two-node.png|800]] *Nodes A and B share a $2\ \Omega$ resistor. A has a $4\ \Omega$ resistor to reference and a $3\ \mathrm{A}$ source pointing upward from reference. B has a $6\ \Omega$ resistor to reference and a $1\ \mathrm{A}$ source pointing upward from reference. The bottom wire is reference.*
?
From the node equations, $V_A=10\ \mathrm{V}$ and $V_B=9\ \mathrm{V}$. Both source currents enter their negative terminals. Thus $P_{3\mathrm{A}}=-(10)(3)=-30\ \mathrm{W}$ and $P_{1\mathrm{A}}=-(9)(1)=-9\ \mathrm{W}$; both deliver. Resistor absorption is $10^2/4+(10-9)^2/2+9^2/6=25+0.5+13.5=39\ \mathrm{W}$. Therefore $39-30-9=0\ \mathrm{W}$.

**8.** Define clockwise mesh currents and solve for them. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-two-mesh.png|800]] *Two clockwise mesh currents are shown. The left loop has a $12\ \mathrm{V}$ source positive at the top and a top $2\ \Omega$ resistor. The right loop has a top $6\ \Omega$ resistor and a $6\ \mathrm{V}$ source positive at the top. A shared vertical $4\ \Omega$ resistor separates the loops; the bottom wire is common.*
?
Let $i_1$ and $i_2$ be clockwise in the left and right meshes. KVL gives $-12+2i_1+4(i_1-i_2)=0$ and $6i_2+6+4(i_2-i_1)=0$. Thus $6i_1-4i_2=12$ and $-4i_1+10i_2=-6$. Solving gives $i_1=24/11=2.18\ \mathrm{A}$ clockwise and $i_2=3/11=0.273\ \mathrm{A}$ clockwise.

**9.** Find the current in the shared resistor and state its physical direction. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-two-mesh.png|800]] *Two clockwise mesh currents are shown. The left loop has a $12\ \mathrm{V}$ source positive at the top and a top $2\ \Omega$ resistor. The right loop has a top $6\ \Omega$ resistor and a $6\ \mathrm{V}$ source positive at the top. A shared vertical $4\ \Omega$ resistor separates the loops; the bottom wire is common.*
?
The mesh equations are $6i_1-4i_2=12$ and $-4i_1+10i_2=-6$, which give $i_1=24/11\ \mathrm{A}$ and $i_2=3/11\ \mathrm{A}$. The shared-branch current, downward as referenced by the left mesh, is $i_1-i_2=21/11=1.91\ \mathrm{A}$. It is positive, so the physical direction is downward.

**10.** Use nodal analysis to find the voltages at L and R. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-bridge.png|800]] *A $12\ \mathrm{V}$ source fixes the left top node. A $3\ \Omega$ resistor connects the 12 V node to L, a $4\ \Omega$ resistor connects L to R, and a separate upper $6\ \Omega$ resistor connects the 12 V node directly to R. L has $6\ \Omega$ to reference, and R has $3\ \Omega$ to reference.*
?
With the bottom wire as reference, KCL gives $\frac{V_L-12}{3}+\frac{V_L}{6}+\frac{V_L-V_R}{4}=0$ and $\frac{V_R-12}{6}+\frac{V_R}{3}+\frac{V_R-V_L}{4}=0$. Multiplying by $12$ gives $9V_L-3V_R=48$ and $-3V_L+9V_R=24$. Solving gives $V_L=7.00\ \mathrm{V}$ and $V_R=5.00\ \mathrm{V}$.

**11.** Find the magnitude and direction of every resistor current. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-bridge.png|800]] *A $12\ \mathrm{V}$ source fixes the left top node. A $3\ \Omega$ resistor connects the 12 V node to L, a $4\ \Omega$ resistor connects L to R, and a separate upper $6\ \Omega$ resistor connects the 12 V node directly to R. L has $6\ \Omega$ to reference, and R has $3\ \Omega$ to reference.*
?
Using $V_L=7\ \mathrm{V}$ and $V_R=5\ \mathrm{V}$, the currents are $(12-7)/3=5/3\ \mathrm{A}$ from the source node to L, $7/6\ \mathrm{A}$ from L to reference, $(7-5)/4=0.500\ \mathrm{A}$ from L to R, $5/3\ \mathrm{A}$ from R to reference through $3\ \Omega$, and $(12-5)/6=7/6\ \mathrm{A}$ from the source node to R. KCL checks at each node.

**12.** Form a supernode and find the two node voltages. ![[../images/Q13-kvl-kcl-interpretation.png|800]] *A $15\ \mathrm{V}$ source feeds A through $5\ \Omega$. From A to reference are $10\ \Omega$ and a downward $1\ \mathrm{A}$ source. A $6\ \mathrm{V}$ source connects A, its positive terminal, to B, its negative terminal. B has $4\ \Omega$ to reference. The reference arrow through the $6\ \mathrm{V}$ source points from B to A.*
?
The constraint is $V_A-V_B=6$. KCL for the supernode is $\frac{V_A-15}{5}+\frac{V_A}{10}+1+\frac{V_B}{4}=0$. Multiplying by $20$ gives $6V_A+5V_B=40$. With $V_B=V_A-6$, $11V_A=70$. Therefore $V_A=70/11=6.36\ \mathrm{V}$ and $V_B=4/11=0.364\ \mathrm{V}$.

**13.** Find the referenced current through the six-volt source, interpret its sign, and classify its power. ![[../images/Q13-kvl-kcl-interpretation.png|800]] *A $15\ \mathrm{V}$ source feeds A through $5\ \Omega$. From A to reference are $10\ \Omega$ and a downward $1\ \mathrm{A}$ source. A $6\ \mathrm{V}$ source connects A, its positive terminal, to B, its negative terminal. B has $4\ \Omega$ to reference. The reference arrow through the $6\ \mathrm{V}$ source points from B to A.*
?
From supernode analysis, $V_A=70/11\ \mathrm{V}$ and $V_B=4/11\ \mathrm{V}$. KCL at B gives $i_x+V_B/4=0$, so $i_x=-1/11\ \mathrm{A}=-90.9\ \mathrm{mA}$. The actual current is $90.9\ \mathrm{mA}$ from A to B, opposite the arrow. It enters the positive terminal, so $P_{6\mathrm{V}}=6(1/11)=6/11=0.545\ \mathrm{W}$ absorbed.

**14.** Use the dependent-source constraint and KCL to find both node voltages. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-dependent-voltage.png|800]] *A $12\ \mathrm{V}$ source feeds node A through $3\ \Omega$. A has $6\ \Omega$ to reference and connects to B through $4\ \Omega$. A dependent voltage source connects B to reference and enforces $V_B=2V_A$, positive at B.*
?
The source constraint is $V_B=2V_A$. KCL at A gives $\frac{V_A-12}{3}+\frac{V_A}{6}+\frac{V_A-V_B}{4}=0$. Substitute the constraint: $\frac{V_A-12}{3}+\frac{V_A}{6}-\frac{V_A}{4}=0$. Multiplying by $12$ gives $4V_A-48+2V_A-3V_A=0$. Hence $V_A=16.0\ \mathrm{V}$ and $V_B=32.0\ \mathrm{V}$.

**15.** Find the dependent voltage-source current and verify the circuit power balance. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-dependent-voltage.png|800]] *A $12\ \mathrm{V}$ source feeds node A through $3\ \Omega$. A has $6\ \Omega$ to reference and connects to B through $4\ \Omega$. A dependent voltage source connects B to reference and enforces $V_B=2V_A$, positive at B.*
?
Using $V_A=16\ \mathrm{V}$ and $V_B=32\ \mathrm{V}$, KCL at B gives downward source current $i_d=-(V_B-V_A)/4=-4.00\ \mathrm{A}$. Thus the actual current is upward and the dependent source delivers $-(32)(4)=-128\ \mathrm{W}$. The independent source absorbs $12(4/3)=16\ \mathrm{W}$. The resistors absorb $(4/3)^2(3)=16/3\ \mathrm{W}$, $16^2/6=128/3\ \mathrm{W}$, and $4^2(4)=64\ \mathrm{W}$. The check is $16+16/3+128/3+64-128=0\ \mathrm{W}$.

**16.** Find the node voltage and both downward branch currents. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-dependent-current.png|800]] *Three parallel branches connect node V to reference: an independent $4\ \mathrm{A}$ source points upward, a $3\ \Omega$ resistor, and a dependent current source of $0.25V$ amperes points downward. Voltage V is measured from the top node to reference.*
?
Take downward currents from node V as positive. KCL gives $\frac{V}{3}+0.25V-4=0$. Thus $(1/3+1/4)V=4$ and $V=48/7=6.86\ \mathrm{V}$. The resistor current is $V/3=16/7=2.29\ \mathrm{A}$ downward. The dependent-source current is $0.25V=12/7=1.71\ \mathrm{A}$ downward. Their sum is $4.00\ \mathrm{A}$.

**17.** Find the power of all three elements and verify conservation of power. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-dependent-current.png|800]] *Three parallel branches connect node V to reference: an independent $4\ \mathrm{A}$ source points upward, a $3\ \Omega$ resistor, and a dependent current source of $0.25V$ amperes points downward. Voltage V is measured from the top node to reference.*
?
Using $V=48/7\ \mathrm{V}$, the independent source current enters its negative terminal, so $P_{4\mathrm{A}}=-(48/7)(4)=-192/7=-27.43\ \mathrm{W}$; it delivers. The resistor absorbs $P_R=V^2/3=768/49=15.67\ \mathrm{W}$. The dependent source current enters its positive terminal, so $P_d=V(0.25V)=576/49=11.76\ \mathrm{W}$ absorbed. The check is $768/49+576/49-192/7=0\ \mathrm{W}$.

**18.** A student claims that current in the linking resistor is $0.500\ \mathrm{A}$ from B to A. Test the claim using KCL. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-two-node.png|800]] *Nodes A and B share a $2\ \Omega$ resistor. ?
The node equations give $V_A=10\ \mathrm{V}$ and $V_B=9\ \mathrm{V}$. Defining link current from B to A gives $i_{BA}=(V_B-V_A)/2=-0.500\ \mathrm{A}$. Therefore the claim is false. The negative sign means the actual current is $0.500\ \mathrm{A}$ from A to B. At A, $3=10/4+0.5=3\ \mathrm{A}$, which confirms KCL.

**19.** Without resolving the circuit, explain how reversing both mesh-current reference arrows changes the numerical answers and physical currents. ![[../images/kvl-kcl-two-mesh.png|800]] *Two clockwise mesh currents are shown. ?
The original clockwise solutions are $i_1=24/11\ \mathrm{A}$ and $i_2=3/11\ \mathrm{A}$. Reversing both reference arrows defines $i_1'=-i_1$ and $i_2'=-i_2$. Thus $i_1'=-24/11\ \mathrm{A}$ and $i_2'=-3/11\ \mathrm{A}$. Only the reported signs change. The physical branch currents, voltage drops, and powers do not change.

## Mesh Questions

**1.** **Basic two-mesh currents.** Find both mesh currents and the magnitude and direction of current in the shared resistor. The left top resistor is $3\,\Omega$, the shared resistor is $6\,\Omega$, and the right top resistor is $9\,\Omega$. The left and right sources are $18\,\text{V}$ and $6\,\text{V}$, respectively, both positive at the top. ![[../images/Q03-mesh-basic.png|800]] *Two adjacent clockwise meshes share a $6\,\Omega$ vertical resistor. The left path has $3\,\Omega$ and a top-positive $18\,\text{V}$ source; the right path has $9\,\Omega$ and a top-positive $6\,\text{V}$ source. One wire joins the bottom nodes.*
?
Define clockwise mesh currents $i_1$ and $i_2$ and sum signed voltage drops. The shared current is downward as $i_s=i_1-i_2$. $3i_1+6(i_1-i_2)-18=0,$ $9i_2+6(i_2-i_1)+6=0.$ Thus, $(9)i_1-6i_2=18,\qquad -6i_1+(15)i_2=-6.$ Solving, $i_1=\frac{26}{11}=2.364\,\text{A},\qquad i_2=\frac{6}{11}=0.5455\,\text{A}.$ The shared current is $i_s=\frac{20}{11}=1.818\,\text{A}\text{ downward }.$ A negative mesh current, if present, flows opposite its clockwise arrow.

**2.** **Basic source-power check.** Find both mesh currents. Determine whether each voltage source absorbs or delivers power, and check total power. The left top resistor is $3\,\Omega$, the shared resistor is $6\,\Omega$, and the right top resistor is $9\,\Omega$. The left and right sources are $18\,\text{V}$ and $6\,\text{V}$, respectively, both positive at the top. ![[../images/Q03-mesh-basic.png|800]] *Two adjacent clockwise meshes share a $6\,\Omega$ vertical resistor. The left path has $3\,\Omega$ and a top-positive $18\,\text{V}$ source; the right path has $9\,\Omega$ and a top-positive $6\,\text{V}$ source. One wire joins the bottom nodes.*
?
Define clockwise mesh currents $i_1$ and $i_2$ and sum signed voltage drops. The shared current is downward as $i_s=i_1-i_2$. $3i_1+6(i_1-i_2)-18=0,$ $9i_2+6(i_2-i_1)+6=0.$ Thus, $(9)i_1-6i_2=18,\qquad -6i_1+(15)i_2=-6.$ Solving, $i_1=\frac{26}{11}=2.364\,\text{A},\qquad i_2=\frac{6}{11}=0.5455\,\text{A}.$ Using passive sign convention, $p_L=-(18)i_1=-42.55\,\text{W}\text{ delivered },$ $p_R=(6)i_2=3.273\,\text{W}\text{ absorbed }.$ The resistors absorb $P_{\text{res}}=3i_1^2+6(i_1-i_2)^2+9i_2^2=39.27\,\text{W}.$ Therefore, total absorbed power is zero: $p_L+p_R+P_{\text{res}}=0.$

**3.** **Dependent-source mesh currents.** Find both mesh currents, the dependent-source voltage, and the shared current. Two meshes share a $2\,\Omega$ resistor. The left path has $4\,\Omega$ and a top-positive $10\,\text{V}$ source. Define clockwise $i_1$ and $i_2$. Sum signed voltage drops. $-10+4i_1+2(i_1-i_2)=0,$ $2(i_2-i_1)+3i_2+5i_1-4=0.$ Therefore, $6i_1-2i_2=10,\qquad 3i_1+5i_2=4.$ Solving, $i_1=\frac{29}{18}=1.611\,\text{A},\qquad i_2=\frac{-1}{6}=-0.1667\,\text{A}.$ The actual right-mesh current is $0.1667\,\text{A}$ counterclockwise. The dependent voltage and shared current are $v_d=5i_1=\frac{145}{18}=8.056\,\text{V},$ $i_s=i_1-i_2=\frac{16}{9}=1.778\,\text{A downward}.$

**4.** **Dependent-source power.** Find both mesh currents and determine the dependent source power using passive sign convention. Two meshes share a $2\,\Omega$ resistor. The left path has $4\,\Omega$ and a top-positive $10\,\text{V}$ source. The right path has $3\,\Omega$, a top-positive CCVS $v_d=(5\,\Omega)i_1$, and a $4\,\text{V}$ source positive at the bottom. ![[../images/Q04-mesh-dependent.png|800]] *Clockwise meshes share $2\,\Omega$. Define clockwise $i_1$ and $i_2$. Sum signed voltage drops. $-10+4i_1+2(i_1-i_2)=0,$ $2(i_2-i_1)+3i_2+5i_1-4=0.$ Therefore, $6i_1-2i_2=10,\qquad 3i_1+5i_2=4.$ Solving, $i_1=\frac{29}{18}=1.611\,\text{A},\qquad i_2=\frac{-1}{6}=-0.1667\,\text{A}.$ The actual right-mesh current is $0.1667\,\text{A}$ counterclockwise. Thus, $p_d=v_di_2=(\frac{145}{18})(\frac{-1}{6})=-1.343\,\text{W}.$ The negative sign means the dependent source delivers $1.343\,\text{W}$.

**5.** **Opposing sources and direction.** Find both mesh currents and the magnitude and direction of current in the shared resistor. **6.** **Opposing-source power balance.** Find both mesh currents. Determine whether each voltage source absorbs or delivers power, and check total power. The left top resistor is $6\,\Omega$, the shared resistor is $3\,\Omega$, and the right top resistor is $4\,\Omega$. The left and right sources are $9\,\text{V}$ and $15\,\text{V}$, respectively, both positive at the top. ![[../images/mesh-B.png|800]] *Two adjacent clockwise meshes share a $3\,\Omega$ vertical resistor. **7.** **Shared-resistor voltage.** Find both mesh currents and the magnitude and direction of current, then calculate the top-to-bottom voltage in the shared resistor.
