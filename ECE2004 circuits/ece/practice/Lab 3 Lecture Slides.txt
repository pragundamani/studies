Environmental Temperature and Power Derating
Ambient temperature
The ambient temperature is simply the temperature of the surrounding air.
For example:
•Laboratory: 25°C
•Inside a piece of equipment: 60°C
•Near a hot power supply: 80°C
The power derating curve assumes the resistor is operating in air at a given ambient temperature.
Surface temperature
The surface temperature is the actual temperature of the resistor body while it is dissipating power.
For example, in a lab:
•Ambient temperature = 23°C
•Applied power = 0.5 W
•Resistor surface temperature = 115°C
Notice that the resistor is much hotter than the surrounding air because it is generating heat internally.
