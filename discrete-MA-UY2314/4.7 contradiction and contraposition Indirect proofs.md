to prove $P(x)$
suppose $\sim P(x)\equiv \top$
prove $\sim P(x)\equiv \bot$
$\therefore P(x)\equiv \top$
##### Prove that there is no largest integer
**Take the negation** there exists a largest integer, suppose it is true
$\text{ then } m\in \mathbb{Z} \text{ s.t. }m\geq n,\forall n\in \mathbb{Z}$
since integers are closed under addition
$m+1\in \mathbb{Z}$
$\text{let } n=m+1, n\in \mathbb{Z}$
$\text{ but } m<m+1$
