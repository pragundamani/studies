##### 3 main operators
1. Negation: `~` or $\neg$ , generally tilda is used but both can be used (Not gate)
2. And/Conjunction:   $\wedge$ (And gate)
3. Disjunction/Or: $\vee$ (or gate)
##### Others
1. xor: $\oplus$ is just not or

#### Order of operations
Not is always first and/or are equal

#### Logical equivalence
Two statements are **logically equivalent** if they share the same **truth values** (identical truth tables)
1. Logical Equivalence $A \equiv B$   
>[!info] DeMorgan's laws for logic
>$\neg(p \wedge q) \equiv \neg p \vee \neg q$
>$\neg(p \vee q) \equiv \neg p \wedge \neg q$

### Tautologies and Contradictions

| $P$ | $\neg P$ | $P \vee \neg P$ | $P \wedge \neg P$ |
| --- | -------- | --------------- | ----------------- |
| T   | F        | T               | F                 |
| F   | T        | T               | F                 |
**Tautology $\top$ or $t$:** $P \vee \neg P$
**Contradiction $\bot$ or $c$:** $P \wedge \neg P$

#### 2.1.1 Logical Equivalences
1. Communitive laws :: $p\lor q \equiv q \lor p$ 
2. Associative laws :: $p \land (q \land r)$
3. Distributive laws :: $p \land (q \lor r) \equiv (p \land q) \lor (p \land r)$
4. Identity laws :: $p \land \top \equiv p$
5. Negation laws :: $p \lor \neg p \equiv T$
6. Double negation law :: $\neg(\neg p) \equiv p$
7. Idempotent laws :: $p \land p \equiv p$
8. Universal bound laws :: $p \lor t \equiv t$
9. DeMorgan's law :: $\neg(p \land q) \equiv \neg p \lor \neg q$
10. Absorbtion law :: $p \lor (p \land q) \equiv p$ 
11. Negations of $\top or \bot$ :: $\neg t \equiv c$
