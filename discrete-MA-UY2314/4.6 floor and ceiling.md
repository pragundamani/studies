#### $\forall x \in  \mathbb{R} , \forall m \in \mathbb{Z} , \left\lfloor x+m \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor x \right\rfloor +m$
Proof: $\text{ Suppose } x \text{ is any real number and } m \text{ is any integer }$
let $n\in \mathbb{Z} \text{ s.t. }n=\left\lfloor x \right\rfloor$
Then by definition of the floor function $n\leq x<n+1$
we add m to all terms
$n+m \leq x+m < n+m+1$, when $n+m\in \mathbb{Z}$ by closure under addition
By substitution, $\left\lfloor x+m \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor x \right\rfloor +m$

#### prove $\forall n\in \mathbb{Z} ,\left\lceil \frac{\left\lceil \frac{n}{2} \right\rceil}{3} \right\rceil = \left\lceil \frac{n}{6} \right\rceil$
###### Even case
$\forall k\in \mathbb{Z} n=2k$
$\frac{n}{2}=\frac{2k}{2}=k$
$\left\lceil \frac{\left\lceil \frac{n}{2} \right\rceil}{3} \right\rceil=\left\lceil \frac{k}{3} \right\rceil$
$\left\lceil \frac{n}{6} \right\rceil=\left\lceil \frac{2k}{6} \right\rceil=\left\lceil \frac{k}{3} \right\rceil$
###### Odd case
$\forall k \in \mathbb{Z} , \quad n=2k+1$
$\frac{n}{2}=\frac{2k+1}{2}=\frac{2k}{2}+\frac{1}{2}=k+\frac{1}{2}$
$\left\lceil \frac{\left\lceil \frac{n}{2} \right\rceil}{3} \right\rceil = \left\lceil \frac{k+\frac{1}{2}}{3} \right\rceil=\left\lceil \frac{k}{3}+\frac{1}{6} \right\rceil$
$\left\lceil \frac{n}{6} \right\rceil = \left\lceil \frac{2k+1}{6} \right\rceil = \left\lceil \frac{k}{3} + \frac{1}{6} \right\rceil$
