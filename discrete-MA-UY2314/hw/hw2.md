### 5. Section 3.3 #56 and #57
#### #56
not equivalent  
$\exists x\in D,\ (P(x)\wedge Q(x)) \to (\exists x\in D P(x))\wedge(\exists x\in D Q(x))$.

counterexample:  
$D=\{1,2,3\}$  
$P(x)$: $x$ is even  
$Q(x)$: $x$ is odd  

The first statement is false because no $x$ is both even and odd. The second statement is true because $2$ is even and $1$ is odd.

#### #57
not equivalent  
$(\forall x\in D P(x))\vee(\forall x\in D Q(x)) \to \forall x\in D,\ (P(x)\vee Q(x))$.

counterexample:  
$D=\{1,2,3\}$  
$P(x)$: $x$ is even  
$Q(x)$: $x$ is odd  

$\forall x\in D,\ (P(x)\vee Q(x))$ is true.  
$(\forall x\in D P(x))\vee(\forall x\in D Q(x))$ is false.
